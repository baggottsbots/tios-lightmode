Honduran Christmas Tamales in Hilton Head & Bluffton

Banana leaf wrapped, made the Honduran way, and the reason a lot of Lowcountry families start their Christmas Eve at Tio's. Holiday tamale orders are opening soon.

By Tio's Latin American Kitchen · November 21, 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer

Tio's Latin American Kitchen on Hilton Head Island and in Bluffton serves Honduran style tamales wrapped in banana leaves, and holiday tamale orders for Christmas are opening soon. Call either location to get on the list: Hilton Head Island (843) 842-8467 or Bluffton (843) 757-8467. Tamales are also on the regular menu year round.

A Christmas tradition from Honduras to the Lowcountry

In Honduras, Christmas means tamales. Families gather in the kitchen in the days before Nochebuena, the big Christmas Eve celebration, to mix the masa, prepare the filling and wrap dozens of tamales in banana leaves. It is a full day of work, a lot of laughing and a table that feeds everyone who walks through the door.

Chef Lynden Zuniga grew up in Honduras on exactly these tamales. When he and his wife Sally opened Tio's, first at Shelter Cove Towne Centre on Hilton Head Island and then at Buckwalter Place in Bluffton, the Honduran tamale came with them. It is one of the dishes that ties our menu back to home.

What makes a Honduran tamale different?

If you grew up on Mexican tamales wrapped in corn husks, the first Honduran tamale you unwrap will surprise you. Here is what sets ours apart:

  • Banana leaves, not corn husks. The leaf keeps the masa soft and moist while it steams and gives it a light, earthy aroma.
  • A fuller filling. Ours are seasoned corn masa filled with adobo chicken, potatoes, green olives and rice.
  • Bigger and heartier. A Honduran tamale is a meal, not a side. Two make a full plate.
  • Naturally gluten free. Corn masa and a banana leaf wrap make them an easy choice for gluten free guests.

Holiday tamale orders are opening soon

Every year more Lowcountry families make tamales part of their Christmas Eve and Christmas Day spread, whether they grew up with the tradition or are starting a new one. Holiday tamale orders at Tio's are opening soon, and the best way to make sure you do not miss out is to get on the list early.

  • Call your closest Tio's and ask to be added to the holiday tamale list: Hilton Head Island (843) 842-8467 or Bluffton (843) 757-8467.

Can't wait for the holidays? Our Honduran Tamales are on the full menu every day: two tamales for $17, served at both locations for lunch and dinner.

How to serve and reheat tamales at home

Tamales are made to be shared, and they reheat beautifully. Keep them wrapped in the banana leaf until you are ready to eat. To reheat, steam them wrapped for about 15 to 20 minutes until hot all the way through, or wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave in short bursts. Unwrap at the table, add a spoonful of salsa and a squeeze of lime, and pass them around.

Pair tamales with rice and black beans, sweet plantains or a simple green salad. For a party, add a gallon of our house made margarita mix from the catering menu: one gallon makes 24 margaritas, just add your favorite tequila.
